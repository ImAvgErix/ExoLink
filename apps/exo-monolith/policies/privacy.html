Exocord friends alpha

Privacy, plainly.

Effective 29 July 2026. This notice describes this specific invited alpha, not every future Exocord deployment.

No advertising, behavioral analytics, or sale of personal information. Encrypted conversations stay encrypted between participating devices. The service still processes the account and traffic metadata needed to deliver them.

Information the service processes

  • Your email address, profile, handle, one-way password and recovery-code hashes, linked Apple identity if used, session and registered-device metadata.
  • Servers, channels, memberships, roles, relationships, read state, invitations, moderation actions, and audit records.
  • Messages and attachments. Ordinary server channels are readable by the service. Encrypted DMs and private encrypted channels are stored as ciphertext, although delivery metadata remains visible.
  • Connection information needed for security, rate limiting, proof-of-work, WebSocket delivery, and voice routing. Edge access logs omit request paths and fingerprinting headers and hash client addresses.
  • Microphone, speaker, and screen-share streams only when you use those features. Operating-system permission prompts provide notice before capture.
  • A single reported message, its attachment metadata and hashes, the report category and detail, and cryptographic verification results when a participant deliberately submits an abuse report. For encrypted messages, the opening secret is used to verify the report and then discarded rather than stored.
  • Platform account suspension and reinstatement records, including the operator, reason, optional supporting report, and time.

Why it is used

The operator uses this information to create and secure accounts, deliver messages and calls, synchronize devices, enforce server permissions, prevent spam and abuse, answer support requests, provide exports and deletion, maintain backups, and meet valid legal obligations. It is not used to build advertising profiles.

Encryption boundaries

Exocord uses MLS end-to-end encryption for DMs and explicitly encrypted channels, client-side encryption for their attachments, and MLS-derived media keys for encrypted voice. The server cannot decrypt that content without a participant choosing to report one message. A successful report makes that selected plaintext available to the operator for review; it does not reveal the rest of the conversation. Server names, membership, timing, room participation, IP-derived security state, ciphertext size, and other delivery metadata are not hidden by end-to-end encryption. Plain server channels are intentionally not end-to-end encrypted.

There is currently no cloud key backup or escrow. Losing every trusted device can permanently lose access to old encrypted history.

Service providers

The base deployment stores attachments on the Exocord server and uses LiveKit for voice and screen sharing. A later operator may enable Cloudflare R2/CDN for scaled attachment delivery; Apple processes information only when you choose Sign in with Apple. Enabled providers receive the limited account, object, or network data necessary for their function and apply their own terms and privacy notices.

Retention and deletion

  • Short-lived abuse-prevention challenges expire after five minutes. Optional email login codes expire after 10 minutes.
  • Password recovery codes are displayed once. Only their one-way hashes remain until a code is used, the complete set is replaced, or the account is deleted.
  • Account, server, relationship, and message records remain while needed to provide the alpha or until removed under the product controls and published retention rules.
  • Scoped report evidence and its resolution record remain while needed to investigate abuse, enforce the terms, handle a valid legal obligation, and age through the disclosed backup schedule.
  • Account-enforcement history remains as an append-only safety and appeal record while needed for those purposes and the disclosed backup schedule.
  • Unattached upload reservations expire after 15 minutes and are cleaned in batches.
  • Account deletion has a 30-day recovery period. At the deadline, identity and session secrets are destroyed and the profile is anonymized. Shared messages remain as Deleted User context for other participants.
  • The server keeps three daily local backup sets before scheduled expiry; protected off-host copies may follow a separate disclosed schedule. Deletion can therefore take time to age out of backups.
  • The encrypted cache on your Windows device is separate from the server account. Uninstalling or deleting the account does not silently erase that local cache.

Your controls

Inside Privacy & interface you can review and disconnect a linked Apple identity, change your password, replace recovery codes, review devices, revoke another device, export a machine-readable copy, and schedule account deletion. Disconnecting Apple deletes its stored encrypted provider token and is refused when it would remove the account's only durable sign-in method. You can also ask the operator to correct information or address a privacy request at {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}. Depending on where you live, you may have additional access, correction, deletion, portability, restriction, objection, or complaint rights.

A platform suspension immediately revokes sessions and current chat/voice access and blocks new sign-in sessions. Reinstatement requires a fresh sign-in; revoked sessions are not restored. Contact {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} to appeal or request access to your data while suspended. Your machine-readable export includes your enforcement history after you can sign in again.

Security and changes

The service uses encrypted transport, file-mounted production secrets, least-privilege containers, a SQLCipher desktop cache, scoped authorization, and verified backups. No system is perfectly secure. Material changes to these practices should be announced to testers before they take effect; changing this page alone is not permission to use existing data for a materially different purpose.
